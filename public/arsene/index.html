About Me

Hi, I am Arsene — founder and software developer at M.A.R.J.E.N.E Software Development, based in Kampala, Uganda. We build. You grow.

I work across web and mobile development — from React, Next.js, TypeScript and Laravel to Flutter, Python and C++ — with a strong interest in API development, software architecture and business management systems.

M.A.R.J.E.N.E is where that craft becomes product: management systems, e-commerce platforms and digital tools designed to help businesses and organizations work smarter. I'm still early in the journey — learning, building and growing the company step by step. If you have an idea worth building, I'd love to hear about it.
